# Branding

Your own favicon and logo on [Pterodactyl Panel](https://pterodactyl.io) 2.0.

## Features

- Upload a favicon for the browser tab. It is served in the page head, so it is right before anything loads.
- Upload a logo and show it next to the company name, or instead of it
- Adjustable logo height (16 to 64 px)
- Your logo takes the place of the Pterodactyl picture on the sign-in pages (on by default; switch it off with "Replace the Pterodactyl picture")
- Works on every page, including the sign-in page
- Fits the ThemesPlus sidebar: the logo uses the sidebar's full width, and the collapsed sidebar shows it alone

## Requirements

- Pterodactyl Panel 2.0

## Installation

Download `branding.zip` from [Releases](../../releases), go to **Admin → Extensions → Install**, choose the zip and
tick **Enable after install**.

## Configuration

- **Admin → Extensions → Branding → Settings**: upload your favicon and logo, and choose where the logo goes.
